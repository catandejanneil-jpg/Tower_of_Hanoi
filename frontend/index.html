How to play

Goal

Move the whole stack of disks from the left peg to the right peg.

Rules

  • Move only one disk at a time.
  • You can only move the top disk of a peg.
  • Never put a larger disk on top of a smaller disk.

Minimum number of moves

With n disks the fewest possible moves is 2n − 1. For example: 3 disks need 7 moves, 4 need 15, 5 need 31, and 8 need 255.

Tips

  • Start with 3 disks to learn the pattern.
  • To move a tower of n disks: move the top n − 1 disks to the spare peg, move the biggest disk, then move the n − 1 disks on top of it.
  • The smallest disk moves every second move, always in the same direction. With an odd number of disks it goes left, right, middle, left, ... With an even number it goes left, middle, right, left, ...
  • Stuck? Press Hint to see the next best move.
